Mukundara Hills Tiger Reserve Resources

This page is a structured research index for people searching information on Mukundara Hills Tiger Reserve (MHTR / Mukundra Hills Tiger Reserve): notifications, management plans, eco-sensitive zone records, government portals, geospatial tools, and biodiversity literature.

Curation policy: primary government and institutional sources first; research papers second; media/context sources clearly marked as supplementary.

How to use this library effectively

  • For legal status: start with notification, ESZ, and clearance documents.
  • For management direction: read Tiger Conservation Plan and Annual Plan/NTCA sanction documents.
  • For ecological understanding: combine field guides, peer-reviewed studies, and seasonal datasets.
  • For maps: use geospatial tools for orientation, never for sharing sensitive wildlife coordinates.
