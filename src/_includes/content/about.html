About & Ethics

A clearer public guide to Mukundara, built with care.

MHTR.in is an independent, non-governmental resource for readable, source-backed information about Mukundara Hills Tiger Reserve. The goal is public understanding without exposing sensitive wildlife locations or turning conservation into noise.

Editorial Principles

What guides this site

Source first

Facts should trace back to official documents, credible research, field references or transparent notes.

Safety by design

No den sites, nests, roosts, live movement points, breeding sites or exact sensitive coordinates are published.

Accessible language

Technical material is translated into plain language while preserving uncertainty, source nuance and context.

Maps with restraint

Public maps show boundaries, terrain, water and habitat context, not risky wildlife-use locations.

Scope

What this site covers

MHTR.in is for public literacy: geography, ecology, documents, safe maps, field notes and biodiversity context. It draws a clear line around tourism sales, official notifications, endorsements and real-time sightings.

Why It Exists

Less noise, more context

Mukundara is often discussed through narrow fragments: tiger status, tourism, isolated sightings or single documents. This site keeps the broader picture together: ridges, water, corridors, habitat, law, research and citizen responsibility.
