About & Ethics

A clearer public guide to Mukundara.

MHTR.in brings together ecology, field notes, public documents, maps and biodiversity context for Mukundara Hills Tiger Reserve. It is written for readers who need the landscape to make sense, while sensitive wildlife locations stay out of public view.

Editorial Principles

What guides this site

Checkable claims

Important facts point back to documents, research, field references or clear editorial notes.

Sensitive details stay out

Den sites, nests, roosts, live movement points, breeding sites and exact sensitive coordinates are not published.

Accessible language

Technical material is translated into plain language while preserving uncertainty and context.

Maps with restraint

Public maps show boundaries, terrain, water and habitat context without risky wildlife-use locations.

Scope

What this site covers

MHTR.in is for public literacy: geography, ecology, documents, safe maps, field notes and biodiversity context. It draws a clear line around tourism sales, official notifications, endorsements and real-time sightings.

Independent public guideUnaffiliated with any government department.
No commercial booking roleNo safari sales, paid endorsements or booking claims.
Conservation-safe publishingBroad patterns only; sensitive data stays unpublished.
Open to correctionsBetter sources and fixes are welcome.

Why It Exists

Less noise, more context

Mukundara is often discussed through narrow fragments: tiger status, tourism, isolated sightings or single documents. This site keeps the broader picture together: ridges, water, corridors, habitat, law, research and citizen responsibility.
